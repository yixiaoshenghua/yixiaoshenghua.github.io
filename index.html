Hi, I'm Shenghua Wan. I work on decision-making algorithms that learn to understand the world.

I am currently an End-to-End Decision Algorithm Engineer at Momenta, Suzhou, China, working on end-to-end autonomous driving.

I received my Ph.D. degree from the School of Artificial Intelligence, Nanjing University in June 2026, advised by Prof. De-Chuan Zhan in the LAMDA Group led by Prof. Zhi-Hua Zhou. Before that, I received my B.Sc. degree in GIS from Nanjing University in June 2021.

Research Interests
